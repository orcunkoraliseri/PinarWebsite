Plate II · Field & Laboratory Competencies

Core Archaeological Practice

Stratified Excavation

Excavation of stratified mudbrick walls, room fills, floors, pits, and installations; section reading, phasing, control sondages to sterile natural, and identification of redeposited material.

Funerary & Cemetery Work

Excavation and full recording of inhumation, cremation, pithos, amphora, and terracotta sarcophagus burials, alongside medieval burial lifting for ancient DNA sampling.

Stratigraphic & Digital Recording

Locus and pail recording in OCHRE, levels, grave cards, finds registers, museum inventory cards, charcoal and flotation sample logs, and daily, weekly, interim, and final site reports.

Ceramics & Technical Drawing

Ceramic washing, sorting, fabric and inclusion description, typology, and spot-dating; diagnostic sherd drawing by hand and digital vector inking in CorelDRAW.

Depot & Collections Care

Finds classification, museum system registration, crate re-sorting across multi-decade collections (1960 to 2025), standardised labelling, cushioned storage, and digital inventory reconciliation.

Regional Surface Survey

Intensive surface survey coordination, daily work allocation, site-code structuring, quantitative find tables and ceramic graphs, and museum archive handover.
