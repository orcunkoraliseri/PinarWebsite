Plate III · Research Themes

Scholarly Focus

Anatolian & Levantine Connections

Settlement stratigraphy, mudbrick architecture, and cultural connections between Anatolia and the Amuq Valley during the Bronze and Iron Ages.

Ceramic Typology & Illustration

Fabric characterization, firing, surface treatment, diagnostic profile drawing, and digital vector inking from Neolithic wares to Classical assemblages.

Funerary Practice & Context

Archaic Aegean and Ionian necropolis phasing, diverse burial containers (sarcophagi, pithoi, amphorae), cremation assemblages, and medieval burial sampling.

Household & Culinary Archaeology

Continuity and change in domestic installations, hearths, adobe ovens, and food preparation from Neolithic Çatalhöyük to contemporary Anatolian villages.
