Assistant Supervisor

2026 season

Tell Tayinat Archaeological Project, University of Toronto, Hatay, Turkey

Research Excavation Square G4.56 OCHRE Database English Reporting
  • Co-supervised the excavation of one square of stratified mudbrick architecture, room fills, floors, and pits, with a team of site workers, under the Site Director.
  • Revised the phasing of walls from baulk sections and opened a sondage to locate wall foundations.

Field Supervisor and Coordinator

2025

İzmir Museum Rescue Excavation, Urla, İzmir, Turkey

Museum Rescue Excavation Third-Degree Protected Site 5 m Grid · 6 Sondages
  • Sole supervisor for the site and its finds on a third-degree protected archaeological site (Turkish heritage designation).
  • Directed daily excavation strategy and stratigraphic recording, and allocated and supervised the work of about eight site workers.
  • Set out a grid of 5 m plan squares, joined the earlier evaluation trenches, and removed modern overburden down to the archaeological horizon.
  • Showed that the only deposit was a redeposited rubble fill with residual finds, then cut six 2 x 2 m control sondages to sterile natural before the site was closed.
  • Identified and dated the finds: a loom weight, a spindle whorl, an Ottoman clay pipe, and pottery from Archaic to Byzantine, including Hellenistic West Slope ware; assessed the study and inventory finds and registered them in the museum system.
  • Wrote the evaluation and final reports for the site.

Field and Depot Supervisor

2025

Klazomenai Excavation, Urla, İzmir, Turkey

Research Excavation & Depot 1960–2025 Archive Pottery Spot-Dating
  • Supervised excavation trenches and grid units.
  • Wrote the daily excavation reports for the supervised area: levels, stone alignments, a hearth and a posthole, bagged finds, charcoal samples, and the photographic record.
  • Described ceramic fabrics, inclusions, firing, and surface treatment, and spot-dated the pottery.
  • As Depot Supervisor, recorded finds data and managed classification and storage.
  • After the season, reorganised the whole depot: re-sorted the pottery crates of every season from 1960 to 2025 by year and excavation area, checked contents against finds slips, standardised labels and numbering, re-shelved crates off the floor with padding for fragile finds, and reconciled the inventory with the digital records, which made finds easier to retrieve and freed space for new material.

Field Supervisor (Cemetery)

2024 to 2025

İzmir Museum Rescue Excavation, Urla, İzmir, Turkey

Museum Rescue Excavation 83 Graves · Phases I–IV Archaic (7th–6th c. BC)
  • Supervised the rescue excavation of an Archaic (7th to 6th century BC) cemetery and excavated and recorded all 83 graves herself, with their grave goods: 27 terracotta sarcophagi, 18 amphora burials, 10 pithos burials, 15 inhumations, and 13 cremations.
  • Recorded each grave on a grave card (plan square, levels, dimensions, orientation, skeleton, and grave goods), and recorded the grave goods in the finds register and on museum inventory cards.
  • Washed and sorted the pottery from the cremation burials.
  • Wrote the daily, interim, and final reports; the final report divides the cemetery into four phases (about 650 to 525 BC) by burial type, level, and grave goods.
  • Protected the enclosure walls and sections at the end of the work with geotextile and about 1,500 to 2,000 sandbags.

Plate III · Field Workflow & References

End-to-End Site Workflow

01 · SITE SET-OUT

Grid & Excavation

Setting out 5 m plan grids, directing team allocations, clearing overburden, excavating stratified mudbrick and burials, and cutting control sondages to natural.

02 · DOCUMENTATION

Stratigraphic & Grave Recording

Locus and pail recording in OCHRE, levels, grave cards, charcoal and aDNA sampling, finds registers, and daily photographic logs.

03 · POST-EXCAVATION

Finds & Ceramic Illustration

Washing, fabric and inclusion description, spot-dating, quantitative tables, hand profile drawing, and digital vector inking in CorelDRAW.

04 · CLOSING

Archive, Conservation & Reporting

Depot sorting and digital reconciliation, museum inventory registration, geotextile and sandbag protection, and final excavation reports.
